Question-  will this mandate that the speed limit has to be 10 mph in all school zones?
Answer-  no the school street is a completely optional program and only applies when a city decides to implement it. It typically only applies to a quiet residential streets next to schools. Question- are cities banned from reducing the size of school zones?
Answer- no cities can reduce the size of school zones, but they would have to have a public hearing before doing so.   Question- is there any cost of cities to implement this law?
Answer- no. There are no fiscal impacts to cities for this bill.  It simply gives them more options.
